# Lockin v1.12.0 — The landing page and the live trial

**Released 5 November 2026**

The product stops being just an installable PWA and becomes a destination:
**[lockin.surge.sh](https://lockin.surge.sh)** is now a full landing page, and
the product moved under `/app/` so the marketing site and the app can share one
origin and one download story.

## What shipped

### Landing page

- New home at the domain root (source in `site/`): hero with a **live 50-second
  Study Mode countdown** running on the Lockin stopwatch ring, screen mosaic,
  showcase slider, how-it-works stage, proof figures, FAQ and footer.
- One domain, three addressable layers: `/` (landing), `/app/` (product),
  `/downloads/` (assets), `/screenshots/` (art).
- Root service-worker kill switch (`site/sw.js`) retires the 1.11.x worker so
  returning visitors land on the landing page instead of a cached old shell.

### Live trial

- The trial is now a real run of the app at `/app/?trial=1`.
- Runs against a pre-filled **demo week** — 14 days of sessions, tasks, schedules
  and a day-by-day log - on a local **Test Account** (`test.account@lockin.app`).
- The trial keeps its own browser store (`lockin.trial.store.v1`), never touches
  the real store, and never loads Firebase or makes a network call.
- One-tap **Reset** restores the seeded week; the trial cannot sign out of its
  demo account.
- Browser-only capability gaps are now stated, not hidden: Control and the
  Settings **Device** card show an explicit **“Only available on the Android
  app”** card with download CTAs.

### Release

- Version bumped to 1.12.0 (versionCode 220); signed release APK, extension zip
  and the packed site deploy follow.

## Install

- **Web**: the landing page, the live trial and the downloads.
- **Android**: download `lockin-1.12.0-release.apk` from the release assets or
  the [public mirror](https://lockin.surge.sh/downloads/lockin-1.12.0-release.apk).
- **Extension**: the 1.12.0 package from the
  [public mirror](https://lockin.surge.sh/downloads/lockin-extension.zip).
