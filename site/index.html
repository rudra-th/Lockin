Questions, answered.

The honest version, including what a browser cannot do.

Trial What works in the browser trial?

Schedule blocks, today and tomorrow tasks, Study Mode sessions, the wake lock, the daily target, the Honesty Log, Progress, export and import. Alarms ring while the tab is open. Everything is local to a separate trial store, so your real Lockin data is untouched.

Android What needs the Android app?

App blocking, per-app daily limits, screen time, usage access, exact alarms that ring when the app is closed, full-screen alarms, and the strict kiosk. A browser cannot see or cover another app. Those cards say “Only available on the Android app” and link to the download.

Account Do I need a Google account?

No. Lockin works signed out and keeps everything in this browser. If you want a mirror on another device, sign in with Google and it copies tasks, schedules, sessions, slips and settings to your own private Firestore. The trial uses a local Test Account and never contacts Google.

Strict How strict is Strict Mode?

Off means tracking only. On, leaving for more than ten seconds is an away episode; you get three free rescues a day, then it is logged. Fifteen minutes of real work earns a neutral exit. The Android kiosk can pin the phone to Lockin and an allowlist, with a deadline that always releases it. It is a focus aid, not a trap.

Privacy Where does my data live?

In your browser’s localStorage. No analytics, trackers or ad SDKs. The Android app keeps its data on the phone; cloud sync is optional and goes to your own Firestore tree. The trial uses a separate key and never reads your real store.

Install How do I install the APK or extension?

Tap the Android download and open the file; Android may ask you to allow installs from this browser once. For Chrome or Edge, download the extension ZIP, unzip it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode and choose Load unpacked. The popup explains every block rule.
