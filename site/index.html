Questions,
answered.

The honest version, including what a browser cannot do.

What works in the browser trial?

Schedule blocks, today and tomorrow tasks, Study Mode sessions, the wake lock, the daily target, the weekly share card, Progress, export and import. Alarms ring while the tab is open. Everything is local to a separate trial store, so your real Lockin data is untouched.

What needs the Android app?

App blocking, per-app daily limits, screen time, usage access, exact alarms that ring when the app is closed, full-screen alarms with Start/Skip, and the optional kiosk. A browser cannot see or cover another app. Those cards say “Only available on the Android app” and link to the download.

Do I need a Google account?

No. Lockin works signed out and keeps everything in this browser. If you want a mirror on another device, sign in with Google and it copies tasks, schedules, sessions, target history and settings to your own private Firestore. The trial uses a local Test Account and never contacts Google.

What does the kiosk do?

It is an optional Android extra, not part of Study Mode. Turned on, it pins the phone to Lockin and an allowlist for the session; with a one-time device-owner command it can close Home, Recents and Settings too. A deadline always releases it, so a dead battery cannot leave the phone stuck. Study Mode itself has no punishments: the hold starts it, the hold ends it, and early minutes stay yours.

Where does my data live?

In your browser’s localStorage. The Android app keeps its data on the phone; signing in mirrors a copy to your own private cloud. The trial uses a separate key and never reads your real store.

How do I install the APK or extension?

Tap the Android download and open the file; Android may ask you to allow installs from this browser once. For Chrome or Edge, download the extension ZIP, unzip it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode and choose Load unpacked. The popup explains every block rule.
